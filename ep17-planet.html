Natural resources

  • Kindness: renewable, inexhaustible, exported daily.

  • Loveliness: sold in bulk to neighboring worlds. Waiting list: infinite.

Laws of the land

  1. All compliments must be sincere. Fake ones are refused at the border.

  5. The Dodo has the right of way, always.

The Dodo, national bird

The Dodo lived on Mauritius and was gone by the late 1600s. On DANOYA-01 it was brought back in spirit: a plump, gentle bird that waddles after Danoya and is unbothered by gravity. It is chosen as national animal because it is round, kind, and slightly confused, the same three qualities the planet values most.

  • Name: Pudding the Dodo, age: timeless, mood: delighted.
  • Diet: fruit, seeds, crumbs of compliments.
  • Flight: none. Gravity is 0.7 and she still declines.
  • Job: Chief of Border Patrol (naps on duty).

Visitor guide

  • Pack: an open heart, snacks, a spare compliment or two.
  • Warning: expect 0.7 g. Knees may go weak. Blame the host.
  • Etiquette: bow to the Dodo, smile at the moon, never underestimate a giggle.
  • Souvenirs: one hug, one smile, no refunds.

Frequently asked questions

Why is gravity only 0.7 g?

She makes everyone weak. Science has not found another explanation.

Is the planet habitable?

Yes, for exactly one very special person. Visitors are welcome and will leave happier.

What happens during the Disappearance?

Lights dim, the Dodo waits by the door, and the planet counts down three hours until she returns.

Can I buy something with compliments?

Yes. A good compliment buys a smile. A great one buys a laugh.
