I SAW THEM.

Danoya, I thought the leggings were the peak. I put a new tier in the gallery. I felt proud of the gallery. Then the grey sweatpants happened.

Soft grey, loose, completely effortless, and sitting on you in a way that should be studied. You weren't dressed up. You weren't posing. You were just you, in the comfiest pants in the world, and my brain left the building and has not come back.

I need the record to show that no outfit has ever done this to me before. They are simple. They are grey. And I have never been so weak for a pair of sweatpants in my life.

Black Sporty Leggings

Condition: changed the curator's life
Ranking: off the chart. A new tier had to be invented.

WOW.

The curator was perfectly happy with the collection. Then this arrived and the whole game changed. Every other piece in this gallery is still incredible, but they now live in a world where these leggings exist, and nothing has been the same since.

Danoya, I need you to understand what happened when I opened that picture. I stopped. I zoomed in. I zoomed out to take in the full picture, then zoomed back in again. I closed it, opened it again just to make sure it was real. It was real.

You were just standing in your room in a t-shirt, taking a quick picture like it was nothing, and you have no idea what you did to me. That curve. The way the tee sits right above it like it knows exactly what it's doing. The leggings that look like they were made for you and nobody else.

And you're telling me it's "not the same"? You're fully dressed in this picture, and it's the hottest thing I've ever seen.
